Returns and damaged items

Unused items: return within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.

Damaged on arrival: report it within 14 days of delivery, with photos, and we'll send a free replacement or a refund.

After 14 days: damage is covered by our one-year warranty. We repair or replace the item, but we don't refund it.

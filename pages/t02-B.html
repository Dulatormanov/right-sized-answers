Team glossary

Owner: People Ops · Last updated September 30, 2026

Words and abbreviations you'll see in Slack and docs.

OOO
Out of office. Set your status and calendar.
EOD
3:00 pm Pacific, when the daily build freezes. Anything merged after that ships the next day. (Not the end of your day!)
LGTM
Looks good to me: an approval in code review.
P0
Drop everything: a customer-facing outage.
DRI
Directly responsible individual, the one person who owns a decision.
